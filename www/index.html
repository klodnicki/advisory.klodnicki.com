Practice areas

Our work is practical, not theoretical: commercial strategy joined with the CRM systems, data foundations, and AI capabilities that modern life sciences organizations run on — for pharmaceutical manufacturers and for the rest of the ecosystem that serves them. Every engagement is led by a principal and staffed by senior specialists in the discipline it needs.

Commercial Strategy

Go-to-market strategy for organizations whose product is ahead of their commercial motion — from positioning and messaging to the market presence that earns a buyer's trust.

  • Go-to-market strategy & positioning
  • Sales organization & leadership design
  • Business-development pilots
  • Advisory for founders & leadership teams

Agentic AI

Practical guidance on where AI agents and automation actually pay off inside commercial and IT operations — and where they don't. Our agentic work is built with guardrails and a human in the loop, so automation stays accountable.

  • Identifying high-value agent use cases
  • Roadmaps from pilot to production
  • Guardrails & human-in-the-loop design
  • Build-vs-buy and vendor evaluation
  • Adoption alongside existing systems

Where we start — life sciences use cases

  • Drug Discovery — agents that help research teams search, synthesize, and prioritize scientific evidence faster
  • Clinical Trials Optimization — agents supporting protocol design, site selection, patient recruitment, and trial monitoring
  • Commercial Effectiveness — agents that sharpen targeting, next-best-action, and field and marketing execution

CRM Strategy

The CRM is where commercial strategy meets the field. We help life sciences companies get more from their CRM platforms — aligning the system to how commercial teams actually sell, engage healthcare professionals, and manage accounts, instead of the other way around.

  • CRM platform strategy — Salesforce, Veeva & the ecosystem around them
  • Platform decisions & migration planning between Salesforce and Veeva
  • Roadmaps that tie CRM investment to commercial priorities
  • Adoption, governance & change management
  • Vendor & implementation-partner evaluation

Data Engineering

The data foundations that analytics and AI products stand on — modern architectures that go well beyond the data lake, with the discipline to keep them trustworthy.

  • Data platform & pipeline strategy
  • Lakehouse, data mesh & data fabric architectures
  • Data quality & governance
  • Support for AI product delivery

These four anchor the firm, from the lab to the market. If your challenge sits outside them, start a conversation anyway — if it’s a life sciences problem, it’s our problem.

Focus

Majoring in life sciences

Buyers hire the specialist. Our team brings three decades of working in and around pharmaceutical and life sciences organizations — across the ecosystem that serves them, and now directly with manufacturers themselves. We know how these organizations think, how their budgets move, and how decisions actually get made inside them. That depth is the point — it's why the work lands.

One thing we've learned across those decades: the product rarely fails. The go-to-market does. Great analytics, great AI — and a commercial motion that can't explain it to a buyer who lives in a completely different world. That's the gap this firm works in.

Open to the right problem, in any industry

Life sciences is the major, not a fence. We take select engagements outside the industry when the problem is the same one: a strong product, a stalled commercial motion, and a leadership team that needs senior judgment applied across strategy, data, and AI.

If that sounds like your situation, the conversation starts the same way either way.

John Klodnicki is the firm's founder and managing principal, with more than 30 years of experience building and leading teams in the pharma analytics and SaaS space. He leads every engagement personally — and draws on the firm's specialists in CRM strategy, data engineering, and agentic AI where the work calls for them.

The firm's motto is Ducere Serviendo — “to lead by serving.” It describes how the work gets done: alongside your team, in service of your mission, and measured by the capabilities and results that remain long after an engagement ends.

Mid-Atlantic Advisory Services is based in Greater Philadelphia and works with companies across the US — a firm small by design and senior by construction, built for the commercial, data, and AI problems that sit between a strong product and the revenue it should be producing.
